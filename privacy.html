MTG Deck Builder privacy

Last updated 15 November 2026

MTG Deck Builder is a free Commander (EDH) deck builder that runs entirely in your web browser. There is no MTG Deck Builder server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

What is stored, and where

  • In your browser: your decks, settings and any custom card art are kept in your browser's own storage on your device.
  • In Google Drive, only if you connect it: decks are saved in one file, “MTG Deck Builder.json”, that you create or choose yourself. Who can see or edit that file is decided only by its sharing settings in Google Drive.

Google account access

If you press “Connect Google Drive”, Google asks you to allow the permission “See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app” (drive.file). With it, MTG Deck Builder can only open the deck library file it created or that you picked in Google's file chooser. It cannot see any other files in your Drive. The app also reads your Google name and profile picture. These are saved in the library file so the people you share it with can see who made each deck.

Your sign-in token stays in your browser for about an hour and is never sent anywhere except to Google. You can sign out in the app at any time, or remove MTG Deck Builder's access in your Google Account under Third-party apps & services.

MTG Deck Builder's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services the app contacts

  • EDHREC (json.edhrec.com) for recommendations and statistics.
  • Scryfall (api.scryfall.com) for card search, images and prices.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr for fonts and mana symbols, and cdnjs for the PDF tool.

These requests only contain card and commander names, never your decks or personal information.

Sharing and deletion

Nothing is shared unless you share it yourself, with Google Drive's Share button or by sending a saved deck file. To delete your data, delete the decks in the app, clear the site's data in your browser, and delete the library file in Google Drive.
